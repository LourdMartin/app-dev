 ## SPIDER-MAN
 Creator
 :**Stan Lee and Steve Ditko**
 
About
:Spider-Man is a Marvel Comics superhero created by *Stan Lee* and *Steve Ditko*. *Peter Parker*, a teenager bitten by a radioactive spider, gained spider-like powers strength, agility, and wall-crawling using them to fight crime in New York City after his uncle's death. He is known for his signature web-shooters, spider-sense, and the mantra that **"with great power comes great responsibility"**.
